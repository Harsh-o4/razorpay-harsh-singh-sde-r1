Objective:
    To Implement intelligent ticket categorization and sentiment analysis.
    Develop an AI agent to suggest pre-written responses or draft initial replies based on ticket content.
    Implement automated routing to the most appropriate support agent or team based on ticket complexity and agent expertise.
    Predict ticket resolution time and potential escalations.

Features:
 ->Acessing keywords as well as deriving the verbal meaning and sentiment behind a ticket.
 ->Access the severity of the ticket based on these parameters along with the amount of money involved.
 ->Store the FAQs and there responses along with the customer feedback rates.
 ->If a ticket resembles an FAQ which has a resolution with high customer satisfaction,the agent should give an automated reply.
 ->Further Escalation of the ticket should be based on the severity of the issue and sentiment analysis of the ticket.
